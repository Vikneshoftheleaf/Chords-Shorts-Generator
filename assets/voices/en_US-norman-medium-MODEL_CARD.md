# Piper en_US-norman-medium

- Voice: Norman, a single-speaker U.S. English male voice
- Quality: Medium, 22.05 kHz
- Model source: [Piper voices, v1.0.0](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/v1.0.0/en/en_US/norman/medium)
- Model card: [Upstream MODEL_CARD](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/v1.0.0/en/en_US/norman/medium/MODEL_CARD)
- Training data: LibriVox recordings; the upstream model card identifies the recordings as public domain.

The voice model and its configuration are downloaded on demand from the pinned upstream revision and SHA-256 verified before use. This project does not redistribute the model files.
